\subsection{Sentence Ranker and Joint Decoding}
\label{sec:sentence-ranker}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{method}
\caption{The evidence model: the span extractor of \S\ref{sec:span-extractor} (grey) plus the
sentence head and its decoders (blue; sequential decoding, \S\ref{sec:sequential-decoding}).}
\label{fig:method}
\end{figure}

The evidence model (Figure~\ref{fig:method}) adds a sentence head to the span extractor
(\S\ref{sec:span-extractor}), sharing its encoder. For each sentence $j$ visible in a window, the
head mean-pools the sentence's token states into $\bar{\mathbf{h}}_j$ and scores it with one linear
layer, $s_j=\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathrm{dropout}(\bar{\mathbf{h}}_j)+b$.

\paragraph{Training.} On each window that shows the gold sentence $g$ (\S\ref{sec:evidence-labels}),
a listwise cross-entropy $\mathcal{L}_{\text{rank}}=-\log\operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf{s})_g$ ranks
$g$ first, and a margin loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{neg}}=\max(0,\,m-s_g+s_n)$ pushes $g$ above one
negative $n$. By default $n$ is mined online: at every step, the candidate (visible, not gold, and
containing no reference answer) that the head currently scores highest, so the negative follows the
model's own confusions. The ablations replace it with a fixed BM25-selected (lexical) or random
candidate, or drop $\mathcal{L}_{\text{neg}}$. The loss is
$\mathcal{L}=\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathcal{L}_{\text{start}}+\mathcal{L}_{\text{end}})
+\alpha\mathcal{L}_{\text{rank}}+\beta\mathcal{L}_{\text{neg}}$.

\paragraph{Ranking and joint decoding.} At inference, each window's scores are log-softmaxed over
its visible sentences, and a sentence's $\log p(j)$ is its maximum over the windows that show it;
sorting by it ranks the passage. Joint decoding adds the evidence weight $\lambda$ times the
log-probability of the sentence holding a span's start to the span score of
\S\ref{sec:span-extractor}:
$\mathrm{score}(a,b)=z^{\text{start}}_a+z^{\text{end}}_b+\lambda\log p(\mathrm{sent}(a))$. We tune
$\alpha$, $\beta$, $m$ and $\lambda$ on validation (\S\ref{sec:ablations}).
