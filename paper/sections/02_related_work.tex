\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}
Extractive QA progressed from feature-based models on SQuAD
\citep{rajpurkar2016squad} and BiDAF's bidirectional attention
\citep{seo2017bidaf} to pretrained readers: BERT, SpanBERT, XLNet,
RoBERTa, and DeBERTa \citep{devlin2019bert,joshi2020spanbert,
yang2019xlnet,liu2019roberta,he2021deberta}. We fine-tune
DeBERTaV3 \citep{he2023debertav3} for span prediction and sentence ranking.

\citet{min2018minimal} select minimal sentence context before reading;
our sequential decoder instead filters existing span candidates without
re-encoding. HotpotQA jointly supervises answers and annotated supporting
facts \citep{yang2018hotpotqa}; our evidence labels come from answer offsets.
DPR uses dense passage retrieval with in-batch and BM25 hard negatives
\citep{karpukhin2020dpr}; we mine the current ranker's highest-scoring
eligible sentence within each window for a margin loss.
\citet{jia2017adversarial} motivate evaluation with distracting sentences.
Our contribution is a controlled study of this combination: negative-selection
ablations, joint versus sequential decoding, and adversarial evaluation.
These components have precedents; performance and robustness gains remain
empirical questions.
